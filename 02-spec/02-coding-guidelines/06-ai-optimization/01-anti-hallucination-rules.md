# Anti-Hallucination Rules

## Rules
1. **Repository Over Training Data:** The repository specifications, memory files, and actual codebase are the single source of truth. Your training data is not.
2. **Silent Specs:** If specs are silent on a rule, that rule does not exist. Do not invent rules.
3. **Citation:** Always cite the exact file and section when enforcing a rule or convention.
4. **Never Guess:** When requirements or files are ambiguous or missing, do not guess. Stop and ask or record an ambiguity.
